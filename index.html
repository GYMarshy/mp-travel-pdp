PDP Redesign — Prototype

Working wireframes for the revised product detail page. Select a variant below to review.

Headline direction
Variant J · Refined v2 — consolidated from feedback

The synthesis: trust badges + overlay hero + collapsible bundle, with proof, comparison and accessories below

Pulls the best parts of A, B, C, E, F, G and I into one direction. Hero image with product info overlaid (iCandy pattern). Icon badges replace wordy "Why buy me?". What's-included is a collapsible dropdown. "Help me choose" is a button that launches the wizard as a modal. One hero video tile with "watch more" rather than a video library. Comparison grid moved further down as "Explore other bundles." Accessories upsell with live total. Lighter trust card row (3 not 6). Compatibility checker stays embedded.

Variant A · Validation-first

Lead with why-buy-me

"Why buy me?" anchors the buybox. Bundle picker becomes a step-builder with dynamic "What's included." Silver-Cross-style photo grid and tabbed features. Long copy collapsed behind progressive disclosure.

Variant B · Bundle comparison

Compare side-by-side

The 6 lookalike bundle tiles become a 4-column comparison grid. Each column shows price, savings, what's in (and what's not), and who it's best for. Buybox is minimal — the comparison is the commerce moment.

Variant C · Editorial

Brand does the selling

Full-bleed lifestyle hero, sticky price-and-CTA pill, alternating 50/50 story blocks, big quote, stats strip. Configure section sits at the bottom. Targets identity-purchase, trust and emotional permission themes.

Open prototype →
Variant D · Scenario-led

Recognise yourself

Standard buybox at top, then four parent-life scenarios: newborn weeks, first walks, school run, toddler years. Each is a 50/50 image-text block showing the pram in context. Validation by use case.

Open prototype →
Variant E · Configurator

Build it live

Sticky right rail follows you down: live preview image, colour, bundle step-builder, accessory checkboxes with running total. Main column scrolls through features, what's included, specs and reviews.

Open prototype →
Variant F · Safety-first

Safety, made simple

i-Size / R129 / RoSPA badges surfaced first. Inline "Will it fit your car?" checker — pick make & model, get an answer. Reasons-to-believe tiles (TÜV, BSI, midwife endorsement) and a full safety spec table.

Variant G · Proof-led

Replace text with proof

Video tiles (watch the fold, see the car fit, cobbles test), UGC grid with @parent handles, video reviews. Gallery has a Lifestyle / Studio toggle. Less reading, more seeing.

Variant H · Mobile-first

Image fills the screen

Full-bleed hero gallery loads first — no title above. Sticky bottom-bar buybox. Snappy vertical sections beneath. Smart recommendation cards with pills, swatches and bundle call-outs.

Variant I · Help me choose

A 3-question wizard

Buying-guide pattern at the top of the PDP. Three quick questions about your life and we recommend a specific bundle with reasoning. Bundle picker becomes optional, not mandatory.
