Description

The Ocarro 2 is our most premium pushchair — designed for parents who want outstanding comfort and capability without compromise. With a generous fully-reclining seat suitable from newborn, an extendable UPF 50+ hood, and all-wheel suspension, the Ocarro 2 handles cobblestones, country paths and the school run with equal ease.

The seat rotates to face you or the world. The handlebar adjusts to suit any height. A spacious 5 kg shopping basket and easy one-handed fold complete a pushchair built to make daily life simpler.

Specifications

  • Suitable from birth to 22 kg (approx. 4 years)
  • Reversible seat (parent or world-facing)
  • UPF 50+ extendable hood
  • All-wheel suspension
  • Puncture-proof foam-fill tyres
  • Adjustable handlebar (95–110 cm)
  • 5 kg shopping basket capacity
  • Compatible with Cybex Cloud T & Maxi-Cosi Pebble car seats

Dimensions & weight

  • Open: H105 × W62 × D94 cm
