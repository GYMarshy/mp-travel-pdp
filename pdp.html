Description

The Ocarro is our most premium pushchair — designed for parents who want outstanding comfort and capability. With a generous, fully reclining seat suitable from newborn, an extendable UPF 50+ hood, and all-wheel suspension, Ocarro handles everything from cobblestones to country paths with ease.

The seat rotates to face you or the world, and the adjustable handlebar suits parents of any height. A spacious shopping basket and easy one-handed fold complete the package.
