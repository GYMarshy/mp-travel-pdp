A pushchair for every chapter

How the Ocarro 2 fits into your life.

Stage 01 · The newborn weeks

Lie-flat sleep, on the move.

Add the carrycot for your baby's first three to nine months. The flat surface supports healthy spine development. The breathable mattress keeps them cool. The hood seals out wind and light when they need to nap.

  • Lie-flat carrycot, overnight-safe to 9 kg
  • Breathable, washable mattress
  • Extendable UPF 50+ hood
Stage 02 · First walks together

Cobbles, kerbs, café-stops.

All-wheel suspension takes the bumps you can't avoid. The reversible seat means baby faces you to settle, or out to see the world. The shopping basket carries everything you've forgotten you'd need.

  • All-wheel independent suspension
  • Reversible parent / world-facing seat
  • Generous 5 kg shopping basket
Stage 03 · The school run

One-handed fold. Fits the boot.

One-hand fold in seconds, with one hand free for the baby on your hip. Folds compact enough to fit into the boot of a Fiat 500, a Ford Fiesta or a Tesla Model 3 — without removing the seat unit.

  • One-handed fold mechanism
  • Folds with seat attached
  • Auto-locking when folded
Stage 04 · The toddler years

One pushchair to 22 kg.

The frame is rated to 22 kg — about four years of use, much longer than typical pushchairs. Adjustable handlebar suits parents of any height. Multi-position recline lets them nap when they need to.

  • Capacity to 22 kg (around 4 years)
  • Adjustable handlebar (95–110 cm)
  • Multi-position recline
